How they were told apart

parser. The tenant writes the day first, and the report came in through the app, by email, or through a booking-v2 form, whose hint asks for DD/MM/YYYY. The shared parser reads every date month first, so day and month swap: a 7 April reminder goes out on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 describes, and the queued fix is for it.

hint. The tenant runs booking-v3. Its form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, people type the month first as told, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same swap, but the fix is in the form, not the parser, so the queued parser fix leaves these reports open. Only reports that came through the form belong here: the same tenants’ app and email reports are parser.

one-day. These dates move by one day, not by a swap. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. A client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and a client in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting. An invite arrives before the booking was made. A month/day swap cannot do any of that; it points to a date passed through a time zone. The desk had already listed these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but the cause table on #4471 still counts them under parser, because their tenants write the day first.

Why the queue hid them

  • Subject lines. Merging on subject joins reports that sound alike, whatever their cause. Different bugs make people write the same words: “wrong day”, “date is wrong”.
  • Intake tags. Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run. The intake tagged as column shows how far the two drift.
  • Repeated rows. The ticket system exports in pages that overlap, so a report can appear twice. Every count on this page is by report id.

For the desk

  • Triage by cause, not by subject. Tenant, channel and booking form decide the cause, and the body can overrule them: a one-day move is never a swap. That needs a new decision record to replace 0001, which stays as it was accepted.
  • When the parser fix ships, check this table again before closing anything. The hint and one-day rows stay open until their own fixes ship.
